# LOCAL-PLAT-CLOUD-TASK-RESUBMIT 预外呼明确异常补提交

**DRAFT — 供讨论与补充，尚未完成本阶段交接**

## 职责与触发

所属工程模块：未填写

功能：FUNC-2002, FUNC-2024

触发：对准确失败原因允许补交的号码显式确认补交

## 前置条件

- 原异常补提交权；明确失败，不是结果未知

- [GR-IDENTITY](../global-logic.md#GR-IDENTITY)

输入：[FIELD-PLAT-TASK-ID](../field-specs.md#FIELD-PLAT-TASK-ID)、[FIELD-PLAT-TASK-FAILURES](../field-specs.md#FIELD-PLAT-TASK-FAILURES)、[FIELD-PLAT-TASK-RESOURCES](../field-specs.md#FIELD-PLAT-TASK-RESOURCES)、[FIELD-PLAT-CONTACT-DECISION](../field-specs.md#FIELD-PLAT-CONTACT-DECISION)、[FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST](../field-specs.md#FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST)

## 处理步骤

### LOCAL-PLAT-CLOUD-TASK-RESUBMIT-S1

按原供应商失败分类与额度筛选；先核对未知旧请求

读取：FIELD-PLAT-TASK-ID, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-FAILURES, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-RESOURCES, FIELD-PLAT-CONTACT-DECISION, FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST；写入：无

规则：GR-IDENTITY, GR-SCOPE, GR-AUTH, GR-UNKNOWN, GR-LIST-ORDER

### LOCAL-PLAT-CLOUD-TASK-RESUBMIT-S2

逐号码重新公共禁呼，不用restrict-tel类补交规则绕过

读取：FIELD-PLAT-TASK-ID, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-FAILURES, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-RESOURCES, FIELD-PLAT-CONTACT-DECISION, FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST；写入：无

规则：GR-IDENTITY, GR-SCOPE, GR-AUTH, GR-UNKNOWN, GR-LIST-ORDER

### LOCAL-PLAT-CLOUD-TASK-RESUBMIT-S3

同请求去重，明确受理与终态分离

读取：FIELD-PLAT-TASK-ID, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-FAILURES, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-RESOURCES, FIELD-PLAT-CONTACT-DECISION, FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST；写入：FIELD-PLAT-OP-STATE, FIELD-PLAT-AUDIT

规则：GR-IDENTITY, GR-SCOPE, GR-AUTH, GR-UNKNOWN, GR-LIST-ORDER

## 分支与异常

### LOCAL-PLAT-CLOUD-TASK-RESUBMIT-B1

条件：前提、字段及对象修订校验通过且结果明确成功

结果：仅允许的明确异常补交发生一次；被禁呼或未知号码不提交

涉及字段：FIELD-PLAT-TASK-ID, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-FAILURES, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-RESOURCES, FIELD-PLAT-CONTACT-DECISION, FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST, FIELD-PLAT-OP-STATE, FIELD-PLAT-AUDIT

验证：CHECK-PLAT-CLOUD-TASK-RESUBMIT

### LOCAL-PLAT-CLOUD-TASK-RESUBMIT-B2

条件：无权/字段不合法/对象失效/修订冲突

结果：明确显示本次拒绝原因，保留可继续的输入和已确认原值，不作变更

涉及字段：FIELD-PLAT-TASK-ID, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-FAILURES, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-RESOURCES, FIELD-PLAT-CONTACT-DECISION, FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST, FIELD-PLAT-OP-STATE, FIELD-PLAT-AUDIT

验证：CHECK-PLAT-CLOUD-TASK-RESUBMIT

### LOCAL-PLAT-CLOUD-TASK-RESUBMIT-B3

条件：提交失败或结果未知

结果：失败保留原确认值；未知显示待核并按同请求核对，不重复提交或假报成功

涉及字段：FIELD-PLAT-TASK-ID, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-FAILURES, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-RESOURCES, FIELD-PLAT-CONTACT-DECISION, FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST, FIELD-PLAT-OP-STATE, FIELD-PLAT-AUDIT

验证：CHECK-PLAT-CLOUD-TASK-RESUBMIT

## 完成后的状态

- 仅允许的明确异常补交发生一次；被禁呼或未知号码不提交
