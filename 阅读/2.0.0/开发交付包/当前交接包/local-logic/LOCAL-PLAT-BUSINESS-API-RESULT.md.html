## 处理步骤

### LOCAL-PLAT-BUSINESS-API-RESULT-S1

仅成功保存的准确人工结果进入回传；待继续跟进不假报完成

读取：FIELD-PLAT-APP-ID, FIELD-PLAT-APP-CALLBACK, FIELD-PLAT-CUSTOMER-ID, FIELD-PLAT-CALL-ID, FIELD-PLAT-PRO-FOLLOW-STATE, FIELD-PLAT-API-RESULT, FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST；写入：无

规则：GR-IDENTITY, GR-SCOPE, GR-AUTH, GR-UNKNOWN, GR-LIST-ORDER

### LOCAL-PLAT-BUSINESS-API-RESULT-S2

保留原请求与回传payload，成功/失败/未知分开

读取：FIELD-PLAT-APP-ID, FIELD-PLAT-APP-CALLBACK, FIELD-PLAT-CUSTOMER-ID, FIELD-PLAT-CALL-ID, FIELD-PLAT-PRO-FOLLOW-STATE, FIELD-PLAT-API-RESULT, FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST；写入：无

规则：GR-IDENTITY, GR-SCOPE, GR-AUTH, GR-UNKNOWN, GR-LIST-ORDER

### LOCAL-PLAT-BUSINESS-API-RESULT-S3

超时先查同请求；重试不重复业务完成、拒联或拨号

读取：FIELD-PLAT-APP-ID, FIELD-PLAT-APP-CALLBACK, FIELD-PLAT-CUSTOMER-ID, FIELD-PLAT-CALL-ID, FIELD-PLAT-PRO-FOLLOW-STATE, FIELD-PLAT-API-RESULT, FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST；写入：FIELD-PLAT-API-RESULT, FIELD-PLAT-AUDIT

规则：GR-IDENTITY, GR-SCOPE, GR-AUTH, GR-UNKNOWN, GR-LIST-ORDER

## 分支与异常

### LOCAL-PLAT-BUSINESS-API-RESULT-B1

条件：前提、字段及对象修订校验通过且结果明确成功

结果：完成结果准确回传或保持待回传可恢复状态，不能以发出请求当对端已收

涉及字段：FIELD-PLAT-APP-ID, FIELD-PLAT-APP-CALLBACK, FIELD-PLAT-CUSTOMER-ID, FIELD-PLAT-CALL-ID, FIELD-PLAT-PRO-FOLLOW-STATE, FIELD-PLAT-API-RESULT, FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST, FIELD-PLAT-AUDIT

验证：CHECK-PLAT-BUSINESS-API-RESULT

### LOCAL-PLAT-BUSINESS-API-RESULT-B2

条件：无权/字段不合法/对象失效/修订冲突

结果：明确显示本次拒绝原因，保留可继续的输入和已确认原值，不作变更

涉及字段：FIELD-PLAT-APP-ID, FIELD-PLAT-APP-CALLBACK, FIELD-PLAT-CUSTOMER-ID, FIELD-PLAT-CALL-ID, FIELD-PLAT-PRO-FOLLOW-STATE, FIELD-PLAT-API-RESULT, FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST, FIELD-PLAT-AUDIT

验证：CHECK-PLAT-BUSINESS-API-RESULT

### LOCAL-PLAT-BUSINESS-API-RESULT-B3

条件：提交失败或结果未知

结果：失败保留原确认值；未知显示待核并按同请求核对，不重复提交或假报成功

涉及字段：FIELD-PLAT-APP-ID, FIELD-PLAT-APP-CALLBACK, FIELD-PLAT-CUSTOMER-ID, FIELD-PLAT-CALL-ID, FIELD-PLAT-PRO-FOLLOW-STATE, FIELD-PLAT-API-RESULT, FIELD-PLAT-REQUEST, FIELD-PLAT-AUDIT

验证：CHECK-PLAT-BUSINESS-API-RESULT
