## 处理步骤

### STEP-FLOW-ARCHIVE-1

有人工必须由实际人先保存内容；纯AI/明确无人由系统保存事实摘要

读取：FIELD-FLOW-ACTUAL-HANDLER, FIELD-FLOW-HUMAN-ANSWER, FIELD-FLOW-CONTENT-SAVED；写入：FIELD-FLOW-CONTENT-SNAPSHOT, FIELD-FLOW-CONTENT-SAVED

规则：GR-DATA-FACTS, GR-UNKNOWN, GR-SERIAL

### STEP-FLOW-ARCHIVE-2

两模式必要真实终态、重试与本人内容明确后唯一归档；TEMPLATE另核配置下游依赖，DIRECT无模板路由依赖且归档后回同租户待跟进，不自动新起呼

读取：FIELD-FLOW-RETRIES-COMPLETE, FIELD-FLOW-ROUTING-EVIDENCE, FIELD-FLOW-TAG-EVIDENCE, FIELD-FLOW-CONTENT-SAVED, FIELD-FLOW-HANDLING-MODE；写入：FIELD-FLOW-ARCHIVED, FIELD-FLOW-ARCHIVE-STATE, FIELD-FLOW-STATISTICS

规则：GR-DATA-FACTS, GR-UNKNOWN, GR-SERIAL, GR-HANDLING-MODE

### STEP-FLOW-ARCHIVE-3

DIRECT沿准确record/owner/followup/attempt和实际办理人处理，同样保本人填写、正式快照、当前值权限及迟到隔离；无run/node不构造假模板身份。TEMPLATE仍核原准确run/node。

读取：FIELD-FLOW-HANDLING-MODE；写入：无

规则：GR-DATA-FACTS, GR-UNKNOWN, GR-SERIAL, GR-HANDLING-MODE

## 分支与异常

### BRANCH-FLOW-ARCHIVE-1

条件：首通结束但重试或人/标签事实未知

结果：保持待结果/待核，不计完成

涉及字段：FIELD-FLOW-ARCHIVE-STATE, FIELD-FLOW-STATISTICS

验证：CHECK-FLOW-ARCHIVE

### BRANCH-FLOW-ARCHIVE-2

条件：SYSTEM未接听自动归档

结果：只记录真实事实，不以继承旧result值造新人工confirmed

涉及字段：FIELD-FLOW-CONFIRMED-FIELDS, FIELD-FLOW-ROUTING-FIELD-SNAPSHOT

验证：CHECK-FLOW-ARCHIVE

### BRANCH-FLOW-ARCHIVE-3

条件：完成或同事件重复到达

结果：不重复封内容或计完成；真实零拨失败只记异常

涉及字段：FIELD-FLOW-ARCHIVED, FIELD-FLOW-STATISTICS

验证：CHECK-FLOW-ARCHIVE
