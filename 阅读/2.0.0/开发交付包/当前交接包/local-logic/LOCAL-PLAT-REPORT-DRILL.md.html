## 分支与异常

### LOCAL-PLAT-REPORT-DRILL-B1

条件：前提、字段及对象修订校验通过且结果明确成功

结果：显示对应Cloud报表授权明细，不因图表可读获取业务写权

涉及字段：FIELD-PLAT-REPORT-KIND, FIELD-PLAT-QUERY, FIELD-PLAT-REPORT-DATA, FIELD-PLAT-CUSTOMER-ID, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-ID, FIELD-PLAT-CALL-ID

验证：CHECK-PLAT-REPORT-DRILL

### LOCAL-PLAT-REPORT-DRILL-B2

条件：无权/字段不合法/对象失效/修订冲突

结果：明确显示本次拒绝原因，保留可继续的输入和已确认原值，不作变更

涉及字段：FIELD-PLAT-REPORT-KIND, FIELD-PLAT-QUERY, FIELD-PLAT-REPORT-DATA, FIELD-PLAT-CUSTOMER-ID, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-ID, FIELD-PLAT-CALL-ID

验证：CHECK-PLAT-REPORT-DRILL

### LOCAL-PLAT-REPORT-DRILL-B3

条件：提交失败或结果未知

结果：失败保留原确认值；未知显示待核并按同请求核对，不重复提交或假报成功

涉及字段：FIELD-PLAT-REPORT-KIND, FIELD-PLAT-QUERY, FIELD-PLAT-REPORT-DATA, FIELD-PLAT-CUSTOMER-ID, FIELD-PLAT-TASK-ID, FIELD-PLAT-CALL-ID

验证：CHECK-PLAT-REPORT-DRILL
