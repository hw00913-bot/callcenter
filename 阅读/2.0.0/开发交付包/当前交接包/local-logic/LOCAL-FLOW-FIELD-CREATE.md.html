## 分支与异常

### BRANCH-FLOW-FIELD-CREATE-1

条件：合法新定义

结果：保存字段库，提示尚未加入分类；不自动产生业务或分类引用

涉及字段：FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-ID, FIELD-FLOW-BRAND-ID, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-USAGE, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-LABEL, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-TYPE, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-KEY, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-ENABLED, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-OPTIONS

验证：CHECK-FLOW-FIELD-CREATE

### BRANCH-FLOW-FIELD-CREATE-2

条件：用途/编码/选项不合法或当前无权

结果：保输入并拒绝，不生成半条字段

涉及字段：FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-ID, FIELD-FLOW-BRAND-ID, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-USAGE, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-LABEL, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-TYPE, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-KEY, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-ENABLED, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-OPTIONS, FIELD-FLOW-OPTION-ID, FIELD-FLOW-OPTION-LABEL, FIELD-FLOW-OPTION-KEY, FIELD-FLOW-OPTION-ENABLED
