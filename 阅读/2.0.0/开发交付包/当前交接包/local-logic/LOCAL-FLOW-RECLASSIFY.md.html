## 处理步骤

### STEP-FLOW-RECLASSIFY-1

只按稳定字段身份复用，缺目标必填由管理员明确补充并预检

读取：FIELD-FLOW-RECLASSIFY-TARGET, FIELD-FLOW-IMPORT-VALUES；写入：FIELD-FLOW-RECLASSIFY-TOKEN

规则：GR-FIELDS, GR-SCOPE, GR-CONFIG, GR-DATA-FACTS, GR-UNKNOWN, GR-AUTH

### STEP-FLOW-RECLASSIFY-2

重核目标schema/route/原record依据，保持业务ID、brand、统一编码和原批次

读取：FIELD-FLOW-RECLASSIFY-TOKEN, FIELD-FLOW-RECORD-REVISION, FIELD-FLOW-BUSINESS-CODE, FIELD-FLOW-BATCH-ID；写入：FIELD-FLOW-CATEGORY-ID, FIELD-FLOW-IMPORT-VALUES, FIELD-FLOW-RUN-ID, FIELD-FLOW-EFFECTIVE-PLAN

规则：GR-FIELDS, GR-SCOPE, GR-CONFIG, GR-DATA-FACTS, GR-UNKNOWN, GR-AUTH

## 分支与异常

### BRANCH-FLOW-RECLASSIFY-1

条件：跨品牌/普通域/重复统一编码/缺字段或token过期

结果：保输入拒，不合并或覆盖其他业务

涉及字段：FIELD-FLOW-RECLASSIFY-TOKEN, FIELD-FLOW-CATEGORY-ID

验证：CHECK-FLOW-RECLASSIFY

### BRANCH-FLOW-RECLASSIFY-2

条件：明确成功

结果：记录转类原因和前后分类/当前值，原运行快照仍按其原分类可读

涉及字段：FIELD-FLOW-CATEGORY-ID, FIELD-FLOW-CORRECTIONS, FIELD-FLOW-CONTENT-SNAPSHOT

验证：CHECK-FLOW-RECLASSIFY

### BRANCH-FLOW-RECLASSIFY-DIRECT

条件：当前业务为DIRECT

结果：拒绝模板判断、推进、转交、废弃运行或模板重开；同租户再次直接跟进沿直接起呼入口，新TASK来源不得启用模板；仅旧无TASK来源历史的后续流程可走独立“启用流程”预检。

涉及字段：FIELD-FLOW-HANDLING-MODE

验证：CHECK-FLOW-RECLASSIFY

## 完成后的状态

- 同一业务新run而非重复导入，owner不自动变
