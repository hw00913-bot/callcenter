读取：FIELD-FLOW-DICTIONARY-ID, FIELD-FLOW-SELECTION-MODE, FIELD-FLOW-QUERY-CONDITIONS, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-DEPENDS, FIELD-FLOW-PARAM-BINDINGS, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-USAGE；写入：FIELD-FLOW-DICTIONARY-ID, FIELD-FLOW-SELECTION-MODE, FIELD-FLOW-QUERY-CONDITIONS, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-DEPENDS, FIELD-FLOW-PARAM-BINDINGS

### STEP-FLOW-INTERFACE-FIELD-2

校能力、一个上游字段到多参数映射、无自依赖/循环及分类依赖闭包，保存定义

## 分支与异常

条件：配置管理资格不足、能力无权/停用或依赖映射不合法

结果：保定义草稿并具体拒绝，不执行取数也不写客户值

涉及字段：FIELD-FLOW-DICTIONARY-ID, FIELD-FLOW-SELECTION-MODE, FIELD-FLOW-QUERY-CONDITIONS, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-DEPENDS, FIELD-FLOW-PARAM-BINDINGS

验证：CHECK-FLOW-INTERFACE-FIELD

### BRANCH-FLOW-INTERFACE-FIELD-2

条件：定义合法且明确保存

结果：只更新接口字段配置，供分类启用与后续新跟进读取

涉及字段：FIELD-FLOW-DICTIONARY-ID, FIELD-FLOW-SELECTION-MODE, FIELD-FLOW-QUERY-CONDITIONS, FIELD-FLOW-FIELD-DEPENDS, FIELD-FLOW-PARAM-BINDINGS
