### STEP-FLOW-CORRECT-1

仅显示当前启用可维护定义，分IMPORT/FOLLOWUP身份和类型校验；号码等允许清空为未知，后续实际起呼仍必须重校有效号码

规则：GR-FIELDS, GR-DATA-FACTS, GR-AUTH, GR-UNKNOWN

### STEP-FLOW-CORRECT-2

按明确字段直接更新当前值及别名一致性，留before/after/来源和原因

读取：FIELD-FLOW-REASON, FIELD-FLOW-RECORD-REVISION；写入：FIELD-FLOW-IMPORT-VALUES, FIELD-FLOW-FOLLOWUP-VALUES, FIELD-FLOW-CORRECTIONS

规则：GR-FIELDS, GR-DATA-FACTS, GR-AUTH, GR-UNKNOWN

### STEP-FLOW-CORRECT-3

DIRECT沿准确record/owner/followup/attempt和实际办理人处理，同样保本人填写、正式快照、当前值权限及迟到隔离；无run/node不构造假模板身份。TEMPLATE仍核原准确run/node。

读取：FIELD-FLOW-HANDLING-MODE；写入：无

规则：GR-FIELDS, GR-DATA-FACTS, GR-AUTH, GR-UNKNOWN, GR-HANDLING-MODE
