## 处理步骤

### LOCAL-CLASS-FIELDS-SAVE-STEP-1

引用已存字段，分别设置顺序、显示和必填，可移除预置引用

读取：FIELD-DEFINITION, FIELD-CLASS-FIELD-REFS, FIELD-CLASS-REQUIRED, FIELD-CLASS-VISIBLE, FIELD-CLASS-ORDER；写入：无

规则：BR-026, BR-027, BR-032, BR-035

### LOCAL-CLASS-FIELDS-SAVE-STEP-2

检查必需依赖可在分类提供；可选缺失仅说明而不强补

读取：FIELD-DEFINITION, FIELD-CLASS-FIELD-REFS, FIELD-CLASS-REQUIRED, FIELD-CLASS-VISIBLE；写入：无

规则：BR-026, BR-027, BR-032, BR-035

### LOCAL-CLASS-FIELDS-SAVE-STEP-3

保存分类新配置供后续通话使用，不改健康本通整版

读取：FIELD-CATEGORY-DEFINITION, FIELD-CLASS-FIELD-REFS；写入：FIELD-CATEGORY-DEFINITION, FIELD-CLASS-FIELD-REFS

规则：BR-026, BR-027, BR-032, BR-035

## 分支与异常

### LOCAL-CLASS-FIELDS-SAVE-BRANCH-1

条件：必需上游未加入/不显示

结果：明确定位所需来源，用户调整后再保存，不自动改其他必填

涉及字段：FIELD-CLASS-FIELD-REFS, FIELD-CLASS-REQUIRED, FIELD-CLASS-VISIBLE

验证：CHECK-111, CHECK-124, CHECK-134

### LOCAL-CLASS-FIELDS-SAVE-BRANCH-2

条件：可选上游未加入/隐藏

结果：可保存；后续按条件无值规则省参

涉及字段：FIELD-CLASS-FIELD-REFS

验证：CHECK-111, CHECK-125, CHECK-124, CHECK-134

### LOCAL-CLASS-FIELDS-SAVE-BRANCH-3

条件：明确空引用集合

结果：允许沿原规则保存且刷新不补；当前有效通话照旧版

涉及字段：FIELD-CLASS-FIELD-REFS

验证：CHECK-146, CHECK-EMPTY-CATALOG
